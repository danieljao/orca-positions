Orca Whirlpool Positions

Enter a Solana wallet address to see its open Orca concentrated-liquidity positions and pending yield (uncollected fees + rewards), read directly from the chain. Nothing is sent anywhere except read-only RPC calls.

Solana's public RPC now blocks direct browser requests, so you need your own free endpoint. Sign up for a free account at Helius, QuickNode, Alchemy, or Triton, copy the HTTPS RPC URL they give you, and paste it above. It's saved locally in this browser so you only need to do this once.
